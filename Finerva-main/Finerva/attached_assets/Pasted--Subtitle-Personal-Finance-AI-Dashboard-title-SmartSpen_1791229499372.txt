Frontend UI/UX personality and time-of-day experience:
- Morning mode: bright, optimistic, energetic visuals with fresh blue-green gradients and confidence-building financial messaging.
- Afternoon mode: balanced, productive, and focused palette with strong analytics, steady card contrast, and practical money-planning emphasis.
- Evening mode: richer, darker premium theme with deeper navy backgrounds, softer ambient glows, and a reflective, calm dashboard mood for planning and review.
- Night mode / low-light feel: dimmed backgrounds, subtle shadows, cooler accent colors, and a more premium, private finance-assistant feel.
- The interface should not feel static; it should subtly adapt its mood and accent intensity based on time-of-day context while keeping the same SmartSpends brand identity.
- Use warm, upbeat tones in the morning and early-day hero sections; keep dashboard cards clear and readable throughout the day; use deeper gradients and softer glows at night for a premium, calm experience.
- Even if time-of-day logic is not fully implemented, the visual mood should clearly suggest morning, afternoon, evening, and night distinctions in the landing page and dashboard styling.
